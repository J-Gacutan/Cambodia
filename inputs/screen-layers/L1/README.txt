These files are from the Global Mangrove Watch and produced at Aberystwyth University. They provide v4.1.12 of the Global Mangrove Watch timeseries, which was released in July 2026. 

The layers consist of annual mangrove extent data from 1985 to 2025, for a total of 41 layers. The mangrove extent layers are provided here in both raster and vector formats. The raster layers (gmw_mng_ext_v4112_stack.tar.gz) are titled GeoTIFF image files with 41 bands; each band is an individual year from 1985 (Band 1) to 2025 (Band 41). The vector layers have been provided in GeoPackage (GPKG) format, with country boundaries burnt into the layers and the associated country added to the attribute table. These files are named gmw_v4112_XXXX_mng_ext_cntry_info_vec.gpkg.gz, where XXXX relates to the year. An Excel sheet (gmw_v4_timeseries_4112_gmw_country_stats_corr_area_formatted.xlsx) has been provided that contains the country mangrove extent (in hectares) for all years and includes the upper and lower 95th confidence intervals for each year and country. Note that these areas have been 'corrected' using the accuracy assessment, which found a small overestimation of mangrove extent at the global level, and therefore a correction factor of 0.9775 was used for all the mangrove extent estimates.

The accuracy assessment for the mangrove extent estimated a global F1-Score for mangroves of 0.9276 (0.9220 - 0.9330) with an omission of 6.357% (5.648 - 7.133) and a commission of 8.093% (7.093 - 9.128). This accuracy assessment was based on 32,569 reference points distributed across 120 geographic sites covering the full range of years within the analysis. Therefore, these accuracy statistics represent the average for the product and will vary locally in both time and geography. Where errors are identified, if they can be fed back to the developers, those errors can be improved in future versions of the GMW timeseries.

The mangrove union (mangroves in any year within the timeseries) and intersection (mangroves in all years of the timeseries) layers have also been calculated and provided for download here. These are available as raster layers (gmw_mng_ext_v4112_intersect.tar.gz & gmw_mng_ext_v4112_union.tar.gz) and vector layers (gmw_mng_ext_v4112_intersect_vec.gpkg.gz & gmw_mng_ext_v4112_union_vec.gpkg.gz). 
 
Raster change layers have been provided for key reference years (1985, 1990, 2000, 2010), but change statistics have been provided for the full matrix of changes (i.e., all years to all years) at the country level. For all the raster change layers, a pixel value of 1 = Gain and 2 = Loss. A value of 0 = 'no change', which might be either mangrove, non-mangrove, or 'no data' regions (i.e., they are outside the GMW habitat mask). The change rasters represent change relative to a baseline year (e.g., 1985), with each band representing change between a specific year and the baseline. For example, the change raster against 1985 (gmw_mng_ext_v4112_stack_chng_cls_base1985.tar.gz) has 40 bands: Band 1: 1986 (change against 1985); Band 40: 2025 (change against 1985).
