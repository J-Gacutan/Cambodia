## Verdict

**Revise.** No finding survives as fatal. Lens B graded its finding B-1 fatal on the ground that the report "never says mangrove soil should stay out of the FRL". The director contests that in part: p. 36 proposes "coastal reference levels, starting with mangroves", with soil, as a system distinct from the FRL discussed on p. 35, and p. 34 calls for mangrove and seagrass baselines "to support coastal wetland accounting approaches outside forest-focused REDD+ and FRL systems". The difference therefore stands. The finding holds as a major: the draft's description of option (a), "avoids reopening the FRL" and "planning-only", attributes words and motives the report does not state. Eight majors change claims, so the verdict is revise, not ship.

## Limits the draft must disclose

- Mangrove deforestation area comes from the FRL's own mapping (2011-2018); global products may show more loss. The significance estimate is indicative.
- Cambodia has no measured mangrove soil emission factor; the choice between a Tier 1 ceiling and a measured factor is open.
