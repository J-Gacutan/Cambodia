# Research note: existing frameworks and criteria for a Cambodia blue carbon scoping (pre-feasibility) screen

> **Intermediate output, not reader-facing.** Desk research, web only, 20 November 2026. No one was contacted. No site-level scoring for any Cambodian site is done here (scope change from the coordinator: no site tests).
>
> **Marks.** **V** = read at source (page or section given). **V-s** = read in a secondary document held in this repo (the CI Cambodia June 2026 report, `../inputs/ci-cambodia-2026-policy-finance-report.txt`, "CI Cambodia 2026" below), primary text not read. **U** = snippet or search-summary only; confirm before use. Page numbers are PDF pages unless stated (for the HQBC Practitioners Guide, Op Manual and Plan Vivo they match the printed page to within one).
>
> **Licences.** Recorded only for datasets or tools the screen could rely on (Section 5, plus the Blue Carbon Cost Tool). No API that takes a contact email was used, so `research-lookup@example.org` was never needed.
>
> **Reading caveat.** Some pages were read through the WebFetch summariser. Where the summary was the only view of a page it is marked V only if the summary quoted the text; numbers in tables were traced to downloaded PDFs wherever a PDF was available (IPCC chapter, VM0033, Gold Standard, Op Manual, Plan Vivo, HQBC guide, GMA guidelines, One Earth).

### 5.3 How the layers combine into go, caution and stop classes

The class logic below follows the MRP Map (loss minus urban minus open water), the KLHK potential-habitat classes, the GMA site test and Jiang 2025. **The class assignments and every numeric cut-off marked "proposal" are analyst proposals for a screening rule, not thresholds found at a source.**

| Class | Rule on layers | Anchored in |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing mangrove (conserve, not restore)** | GMW v4 extent in the latest epoch. Condition (dense, medium, sparse) from canopy cover (KLHK classes: 70-90, 30-70, 0-30%) or the GMW change layer | KLHK PMN 2021 (V); route is REDD+, not VM0033 (VM0033 p11-12) |
| **GO: restorable (biophysical)** | Mangrove present in a **historic GMW epoch** (1985 to 1996 baseline) and **not mangrove now**; current cover is **aquaculture pond** (CAP or Jiang-type pond map), **open or bare land**, **accreted land**, or **abraded mangrove**; **not built-up** in WorldCover, Dynamic World or GHSL; **not permanent open water** (Murray tidal flat or water mask); elevation inside the intertidal envelope | KLHK five potential-habitat classes (V); MRP Map base map (V); GMA p47 (V); Jiang 2025 (V) |
| **GO (stronger): abandoned or low-profit ponds** | Pond with long inactivity or low inundation signal; VM0033 applicability 3(a)(i)-(ii) can then be met | VM0033 p10 (V); Jiang p6 (V) |
| **CAUTION** | (a) Active aquaculture (displacement and leakage; VM0033 3b or 3c must be argued); (b) the parcel lies in a Marine Fisheries Management Area, CFi area, CPA or protected-area zone (permits, tenure); (c) historic mangrove but elevation uncertain (DEM is a surface model; tidal datum missing); (d) seagrass parcels (no approved methodology, mapping incomplete); (e) overlap with Southern Cardamom, Mangrove REDD+ or ARR project areas; (f) parcel smaller than the proposed minimum (**proposal: under 100 ha for restoration, the smallest size in the BCCT scenarios**) | VM0033 p10-11 (V); Op Manual (V); CI Cambodia 2026 pp 31-32 (V-s); ICVCM page (V) |
| **STOP (biophysical)** | Built-up now (WorldCover class 50, Dynamic World "built", GHSL, WSF) or converted to urban; **permanent open water or sub-tidal where mangrove never occurred**; site entirely below mean sea level or above highest tide; outside the GMW habitat mask | MRP Map (V); GMA p47 (V); GMW habitat mask (V) |
| **STOP (legal)** | Core zone of a protected area (access prohibited under Art 11, if the 2008 Law still applies); no pathway to management rights; consent withheld | PA Law Art 11 (V); HQBC p25, p138 (V); Plan Vivo 2.6 (V) |
| **Not mappable in the screen** | Owner willingness, opportunity cost, microtopography, tenure and permits, social licence | Jiang p6 (V); MRP report (V). These stay in the non-spatial criteria (Section 7) |

### Assumptions made

1. The HQBC "2025 Practitioner's Guide" is read as the **second edition dated 2026**, which is the current online version; the 2025 item is the executive summary of 1.0.
2. Cambodia is treated as tropical wet for IPCC Tier 1 biomass (climate zone not checked).
3. The Op Manual's positive list is the NDC 2020 update as written; NDC 3.0 may change it. Not resolved.
4. Where a primary legal text could not be read (ENR Code, Fisheries Law 2025), the CI Cambodia 2026 report's Annex 3 is cited and marked V-s.
5. All numeric go/caution/stop cut-offs marked "proposal" are analyst suggestions. Sourced rules are the VM0033 applicability conditions, the Op Manual's eight criteria and its "any minus score is disqualifying" rule, Plan Vivo's 60%, GS size classes, the GMA tidal-frame test and the MRP exclusion of urban and open-water areas.
6. News items were excluded as evidence. Two findings reached us only through news (the 39% Kampot seagrass decline; some Indonesian figures) and are not used; Indonesian PMN figures come from the KLHK pages.
