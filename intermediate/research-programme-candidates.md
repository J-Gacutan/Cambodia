# T3 research: candidate programmes behind "seed grant and country package scoping for blue carbon" (CI Cambodia)

Date of research: 20 November 2026. Desk-based, web only. No one was contacted; no email was sent to any service.

**Status of this note.** Intermediate research, not a reader-facing draft. Gates: no hostile red team and no director gate have been run. No verify pass on tables beyond re-reading each cited page once. Several pages were read through the fetch tool's summariser, so quotes marked "(as returned)" should be eyeballed on the live page before they go into any client text.

**Evidence marks.** V = the funder's or host's own page or document was fetched and read in this session. U = snippet, aggregator or secondary source only. News stories are used only as pointers (flagged "pointer").

## Bottom line

The phrase "seed grant" plus "country package" is the exact vocabulary of one programme: the **Seed Fund for Country Packages for Forests, Nature and Climate**, hosted and administered by Conservation International itself and linked to the Forest and Climate Leaders' Partnership (FCLP). Its own site lists **Cambodia as a focus country** and states "The Country Package is currently in the Design phase" (V). Its Phase 1 (2026-2027) is "early-stage planning and preparation", which is what "country package scoping" would mean. The programme also fits the "REDD+ scoping" half of the T3 folder name (forest and irrecoverable-carbon focus; mangroves are named in the Seed Fund's launch text). No public page says blue carbon is in scope for Cambodia's package, so that link is an inference (see section 3).

Second most likely is an internal CI blue climate or IBCI funding line (no public programme found). Third is the Global Mangrove Alliance "Mobilizing the Mangrove Breakthrough" seed funding, where Cambodia is not yet named.

Fit-score key: High = Cambodia named and programme vocabulary matches; Med = Cambodia plausible, mechanism fits, no Cambodia evidence; Low = closed, other geography, wrong instrument, or ended.

## 2. What a "seed grant" and "country package scoping" deliverable would contain (top 3)

### 2.1 Seed Fund for Country Packages (rank 1)

What the funder's own pages say (V, as returned by the fetch tool):

- Phase 1, "Design of the Country Packages (2026-2027)": "We support early-stage planning and preparation, including: Concept development; Technical design; Needs assessments; Governance & institutional set-up; Stakeholder engagement; Vision & communication; Costing & donor mapping." Source: https://seedfundfornature.org/our-work
- Phase 2, "Implementation (2027-2031)": grantmaking on institutional capacity-building; "vital reserves of high biodiversity and irrecoverable carbon"; scientific research guiding conservation decisions; long-term financing mechanisms and enabling conditions for investor confidence.
- Launch text (CI, 9 Dec 2023): "initially up to EUR 8 million per Country Package, depending on the potential to protect carbon and biodiversity and the package's ambition and maturity". Seed funding covers capacity building and on-the-ground delivery, conservation of carbon and biodiversity reserves, scientific and monitoring work, long-term finance, and "feasibility studies for larger packages". Eligible recipients: research and education institutions, development agencies, government bodies, civil society organisations. Mangrove ecosystems are named; the Republic of Congo work includes mangrove mapping. Source: CI press page above.
- Grants page: awards come by open call or direct invitation. Applicants must be a CSO, public institution or research organisation authorised to receive charitable grants in the host country; align with the Fund's priorities (capacity, ecosystem protection, community support, knowledge, finance); show sound fund management (financial and compliance reviews, safeguards screening, AML/CFT checks); and comply with the Fund's environmental and social safeguards (stakeholder engagement, gender inclusion, grievance mechanism). Grant size: not stated. Source: https://seedfundfornature.org/grants
- A Country Package is described by FCLP as a coordinated, cross-sectoral approach where governments align policies, institutions and investments; the FCLP 2025 annex calls them "Country-Led", with inter-ministerial coordination "to ensure full government ownership" (PDF p. 5). [Corrected 20 Nov 2026, ywdp.5: "government designed and led" is not on the country-packages page.] FCLP offers "Country Package Overview" documents in English, French and Spanish (not read). Source: https://forestclimateleaders.org/news-and-resources/country-packages/

Worked example of a Seed Fund-funded scoping component (V): Conservation Strategy Fund Colombia, 1 May to 31 Oct 2026, financial-tools component of Colombia's package. Deliverables: diagnostic of existing financial mechanisms; assessment of financing gaps in prioritised initiatives; preliminary design of at least four financial instruments (one prioritised initiative was mangrove conservation); financial modelling, scenario analysis and risk assessment validated with priority projects; a resource mobilisation package with project sheets, donor presentation, financing pathways and partner engagement templates. Source: https://www.conservation-strategy.org/node/438559

**Inferred skeleton for a Cambodia scoping note (U, inference from the Phase 1 list; no funder template was readable).** (1) Vision and ambition: forest, REDD+ and blue carbon outcomes the package would serve. (2) Needs assessment and gap analysis, drawing on the CI Cambodia 2026 policy and finance report (institutions, REDD+ Taskforce roles, tenure, seagrass gap). (3) Governance and institutional set-up: MoE Department of Climate Change lead, REDD+ and carbon-credit secretariats, FCLP link. (4) Stakeholder engagement plan, including IPLC and community fisheries. (5) Pipeline of priority initiatives (mangrove protection and restoration, nested REDD+, ESF and PES). (6) Costing and donor mapping, with links to TFFF, GCF REDD+ results-based payments, ADB/AFD SCMF, Singapore Article 6. (7) Safeguards screening and grievance route. (8) Work plan and budget for Phase 1.

**Template status.** The one downloadable application pack (Peru, Spanish concept note) returned 403 twice. Steps for the user: open https://seedfundfornature.org/grants, click the application-pack link under the Huanuco call, save to `T3 - Cambodia blue carbon seed grant and REDD+ scoping/inputs/seed-fund-peru-concept-note-pack.pdf`. It is a template for the concept-note format only; Cambodia's route is probably direct invitation, so CI Cambodia should supply the actual format.

### 2.2 CI Blue Climate / IBCI / EROL funding (rank 2)

No funder guidance exists publicly. What is documented:

- Scope of the CI Cambodia FY26 study (V, tender page; ToR ref CIC-2025-06-TOR, not read): stakeholder mapping; policy and legal gap analysis across climate, biodiversity, fisheries and coastal development; finance analysis of carbon and non-carbon options, including a preliminary analysis of how blue carbon habitats could be included in the "jurisdictional/nested REDD+ framework". Priorities from the April 2024 Kampot workshop: national blue carbon monitoring system, better data, stronger policy framework, a National Blue Carbon Strategy/Roadmap, benefit-sharing planning.
- Follow-on asks in CI's own 2026 report (V, local file): MoE to build a harmonised national blue carbon map, run national carbon stock assessments and lab capacity, extend REDD+ Taskforce and Secretariat mandates to blue carbon (seagrass), build a phased pathway into nesting and Article 6; NGOs to "design a project to mobilize funding to support capacity building for a carbon stock assessment and the development of an integrated, centralized, and harmonized national blue carbon map."
- A seed grant here would plausibly fund that project design. Deliverable shape (U): short concept note, theory of change, budget, safeguards check; size unknown (BCAF readiness grants of AUD 169k to 776k are the nearest public benchmark, V).

### 2.3 Mobilizing the Mangrove Breakthrough (rank 3)

- Funder wording (V): seed funding "to develop propositions for programs at scale in an initial set of priority countries"; coordinated by Wetlands International with the Mangrove Breakthrough Secretariat; supported by the Bezos Earth Fund. Propositions are available on request from Wetlands International (contact listed on the page; not used).
- What a proposition contains (U, search snippet of Wetlands text): priority landscapes, implementation partners, governance arrangements and pathways to measurable outcomes. Related tools named by GMA (V): Financial Roadmap, Mangrove Catalytic Facility, Regional Readiness Reports, NDC Task Force support for investment planning.
- No grant size, template or application route is public.
