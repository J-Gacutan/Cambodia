## Decisions

- **Programme: the Seed Fund for Country Packages for Forests, Nature and Climate, hosted by CI.** The user confirmed it. Cambodia's Country Package is in its Design phase, and Phase 1 (2026–27) is early-stage planning. The Seed Fund names no blue carbon, so getting mangroves and seagrass into the package is part of the job.
- **REDD+ line: two tracks.** (a) Strengthen mangroves inside REDD+: a distinct forest stratum, soil carbon, a revised reference level, the revised National REDD+ Strategy, and nesting rules. (b) Seagrass on a separate coastal-wetland track that shares REDD+ institutions, safeguards, the registry and benefit sharing, but not REDD+ accounting or results-based payments. The notes say plainly where this qualifies CI Cambodia's report, which recommends extending the REDD+ Taskforce mandates to seagrass. The user ruled this over following the report as written, or giving an options menu with no pick.
- **Framework form: stage-gate.** Stages run idea → pre-feasibility → feasibility → design, with go / caution / stop criteria tied to Cambodian law and institutions, plus project selection criteria. **No site tests**, because the user ruled them out.
- **Spatial screen: method spec and data list only, not a map.** It replicates the Indonesian restoration-potential approach with GMW time series and urban and aquaculture expansion over time. The build becomes a costed activity the seed grant could fund.
- **Non-carbon elements carry weight.** Coastal protection, fisheries, livelihoods, biodiversity, adaptation and non-market finance are screening criteria and value streams, not an afterthought. Anchors: the IUCN Global Standard for NbS, and CI and Rare's *Beyond Carbon Credits*.
- **Audience: internal .md notes for Jordan,** not CI-facing Word documents.
