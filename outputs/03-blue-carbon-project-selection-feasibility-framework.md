# Note 3: Blue carbon project selection and feasibility framework for Cambodia

## 1. Bottom line

1. Run mangrove and seagrass candidates through four gates (idea, pre-feasibility, feasibility, design and authorisation), all inside phase 1 of Cambodia's Article 6 Operations Manual. An optional Letter of No Objection (LoNO) closes pre-feasibility; a Letter of Authorisation (LoA) closes design. Gates are a screening aid over the manual, not a National Authority procedure; the manual governs Article 6 authorisation only, so elsewhere LoNO and LoA are recommended practice.
2. Stops before any score: wrong method (Verra VM0033 excludes REDD (reducing emissions from deforestation and forest degradation), so standing-mangrove conservation needs a REDD route such as VM0048); a core zone (access prohibited under the 2023 Environment and Natural Resources (ENR) Code Art. 364; the 2008 Protected Areas (PA) Law is abrogated by ENR Art. 864); consent withheld; a minus on the manual's sustainable development criteria; or an Article 6 transfer off the positive list.
3. Non-carbon value is a scored group with thresholds; a project can pass on a non-market route with no credit. On four stylised archetypes with proposed weights (illustrative), community-fishery (CFi) mangrove conservation leads under balanced and non-carbon-heavy weights; under carbon-heavy weights there is no clear leader.
4. The first pre-feasibility filter is the restoration-potential map in [Note 3b](03b-restoration-potential-screen-method.md).

**Status:** evidence to 22 November 2026; internal working note for Jordan Gacutan, not for forwarding; desk-based, no named site scored. Gates: verify, hostile red team, director; PA Law/ENR and VM0048 allocation under ywdp.7; positive-list reading aligned with Note 2 (ywdp.11). ## 2. Stage-gates

The Article 6 Operations Manual ("OM") splits the cycle into pre-implementation, implementation, and issuance/transfer or cancellation (OM printed p. 17). Pre-implementation opens with an optional LoNO (concept note, p. 19) and ends with an LoA (validation report, Table 5, p. 21). Non-forest projects go to the Carbon Crediting Secretariat (CCS); forest projects, including mangrove (forest under the 2002 Forestry Law), to the REDD+ Taskforce Secretariat (RTS) (CI report pp. 12, 16). The CI report reads the manual as wider carbon governance (p. 25); that reach is unsettled. Permits bind every project.

Bodies: Ministry of Environment (MoE) and Department of Climate Change (DCC); Marine Fisheries Management Area (MFMA); Community Protected Area (CPA); Marine National Park (MNP); General Directorate of Natural Protected Areas (GDNPA) with General Directorate of Local Communities (GDLC); General Directorate of Fisheries (GDF, formerly Fisheries Administration (FiA)); National and Provincial Committees for Coastal Management and Development (NCCMD, PCCMD); Ministry of Land Management, Urban Planning and Construction (MLMUPC); Royal Government of Cambodia (RGC). No Cambodian cost/time figures; Blue Carbon Cost Tool (BCCT) origination up to 4 years before implementation; OM 60 working days from authorisation request (5+10+30+5+10).

| Gate (OM phase) | Evidence to pass | Go / caution / stop | Body | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Idea (before submission) | One page: habitat; activity; area/province; jurisdiction (MoE PA, MFMA or other); route; partner (CFi or CPA) | Go: fits a third Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC 3.0) measure or National Biodiversity Strategy and Action Plan (NBSAP) 2026-2030 Target 3, named partner. Caution: route or jurisdiction unclear. Stop: plants on mudflat or seagrass, or site built-up | DCC (NDC fit) | CI report p. 12; Seed Fund pages (V) |

Caution: CAUTION class, overlap, or seagrass. Stop: STOP class, core zone, or Article 6 transfer off the positive list | Proponent; LoNO to CCS or RTS (mangrove) (OM p. 19; CI report p. 16) | High-Quality Blue Carbon (HQBC) PDF p. 26; Beyond Carbon Credits (BCR) pp. 7-8 (V) |
| 3. Feasibility | Engagement plan; free, prior and informed consent (FPIC) opened; gender analysis and safeguards; risk assessment; Tier 2 plots; elevation/tidal survey; financial model; tenure pathway; permit applications; coastal committee review; monitoring plan | Go: every item present, no stop. Caution: caution with dated fix. Stop: any criterion at stop (section 3) | GDNPA+GDLC (MNP); GDF (MFMA/outside PA); NCCMD/PCCMD; MLMUPC (tenure) | HQBC PDF p. 26; CI report pp. 10-11, 13 (V) |
| 4. Design and authorisation | Validated design document; permits; benefit-sharing; grievance mechanism; LoA with unit distribution plan (OM procedural term, not statutory carbon right; ownership to authorised participants at issuance; RGC may claim up to 10%); removals period 15 years renewable twice. Non-market: permits, management plan, funding | Go: LoA issued, or permits and funding on non-market route. Caution: fee schedule unset. Stop: LoA refused, or a minus score | National Authority (MoE) via CCS/RTS and Technical Review Panel; permits as gate 3 | OM pp. 5, 11, 21 (V) |

After gate 4, a Letter of Positive Examination is mandatory before units issue as authorised (OM p. 23).

## 3. Criteria

A stop halts the project at the current gate whatever its score; a caution carries a named fix. "Proposal" marks my cut-off; no source states it.

## 4. Selection rubric and archetype test

Score only projects with no stop. Groups 1–5 (5 = go on every applicable criterion; 4 = one caution; 3 = go on most; 2 = caution on most; 1 = caution throughout with a weak route; n/a and N7-style defaults omitted); total = weighted sum / 100; ties if under 0.10 apart. Weight sets are proposals; balanced is default.

| Weight set | Carbon | Ecological | Legal | Social | Non-carbon | Finance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon-heavy | 35 | 15 | 15 | 10 | 10 | 15 |
| Balanced (default) | 20 | 15 | 20 | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Non-carbon-heavy | 10 | 15 | 15 | 20 | 30 | 10 |

What moves:
- **(ii) leads under balanced and non-carbon-heavy** on cost and non-carbon value; under balanced weights no single one-point change displaces it.
- **Carbon-heavy: no leader.** (ii), (i) and (iv) within 0.05; 39 of 44 one-point changes reorder the top or tie it. Sweep with other groups balanced: (i) and (iv) pass (ii) at carbon weight 40% (3.29 and 3.19 vs 3.16).
- **(i): strongest carbon, weakest finance;** tied last under balanced weights. **(iii)** only on the section 3.5 non-market rule. **(iv)** held by its legal score.

Gates matter more than ranking: (ii) needs the REDD route through RTS and nesting; (i) needs pond tenure and co-finance; one of each through pre-feasibility hedges both. Scores are illustrative, not site findings. Verify (`../intermediate/path-c-verify.md`) checks arithmetic and a one-point perturbation, not judgements.

## 5. Restoration-potential screen (summary of Note 3b)

Full method: [Note 3b](03b-restoration-potential-screen-method.md) (governs if they differ). Feeds E1 at gate 2. Follows Indonesia's 2021 potential-habitat layer (756,183 ha, ponds 631,802 ha) and the MRP map (Worthington and Spalding 2018).

**Classes (Note 3b s4, first match).** STOP: built-up; open water; outside tidal frame. GO: inactive pond; open or accreted land. CAUTION: tidal flat; active pond; other converted use; history unproven. Existing mangrove = conservation (REDD); degradation not mapped. Flags: permit gate (MNP, MFMA, other PA, Ramsar, CFi or CPA); legal STOP for mapped core zone (ENR Art. 364); project overlap; elevation check (go→caution); small (<100 ha, proposal). Pond accuracy fail merges classes 5–8 to "CAUTION: converted, use unknown". Baseline 1996; ~59 person-days. Seagrass analogue (known meadow, documented loss, no data) never returns go (CI report p. 33). Tenure, willingness, opportunity cost and social licence stay in section 3.
