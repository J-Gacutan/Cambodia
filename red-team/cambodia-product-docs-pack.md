## Lenses

### Concept validity (strongest tier)

You are a senior scientist in the field the draft claims. Ask whether the thing measured is the thing named. Separate a property of the system (what the environment can sustain) from a choice about it (how a total is shared among users). Check that intermediate and final quantities are not added together, that stocks and flows are not confused, and that a proxy is declared as a proxy. Fatal: the headline output measures a different concept from the one in its title.

For this draft, test claims that this folder is a "product", is "self-contained", that the screen is "national", that modules are independently usable, and that SEEA / ocean-account language matches what the screen actually produces.

### Planning and legal framework (strongest tier)

You are the legal officer of the authority that would adopt or contest this draft. Test every term of art against its statutory definition and every cited instrument for currency: is it in force, superseded or revoked? Ask who has the mandate to set what the draft sets, and whether the draft claims a power its author or client does not hold. Check that plan boundaries are treated as overlays, not as the units the analysis rests on, unless the law says otherwise. Fatal: the output is labelled as a legal quantity it does not satisfy, or rests on a revoked instrument.

For this draft, test jurisdiction naming (MoE / GDF / FiA / MAFF), FLAG and redistribution rules, research-versus-credit licence paths, and any claim that would let a reader treat the screen or notes as government or CI policy.

### Client official reading it cold (mid tier)

You are a senior official who receives this with no briefing and ten minutes. Ask: what is this asking me to do, and can I defend it to my minister? Flag repository paths, internal jargon, "the research says" without a source, foreign terms where a national term exists, illustrative figures not labelled as such, and any sentence a journalist could lift as "the capacity of X is N". Check that an illustrative label appears once per table, not in every cell. Fatal: the official would take an illustrative or provisional figure as a confirmed one.

Here the "official" is Jordan or a cold agent session deciding next build or migration steps from status tables alone.

### Data provenance (mid tier)

You are the data custodian's auditor. For up to five figures, check that the cited source says what the draft claims, at the page or layer cited. Flag figures from news reports where a primary or official source exists, a global product used where a national government source covers the area, a source that is superseded, a licence that forbids the use, and a figure that originates from the draft's own earlier output. Report the figures you did not check as unchecked. Fatal: a headline figure comes from a revoked or misattributed source.
